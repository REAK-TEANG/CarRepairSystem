# Auto Repair Shop Management System - Requirements

## 1. User Authentication
*   User Login
*   Logout
*   Change Password
*   User Profile
*   Role-Based Access Control
    *   Admin
    *   Service Advisor
    *   Mechanic
    *   Cashier

## 2. Dashboard
*   Total Customers
*   Total Vehicles
*   Active Repair Jobs
*   Completed Repairs
*   Today’s Appointments
*   Available Mechanics
*   Low Spare Parts Alert
*   Today’s Revenue
*   Monthly Revenue
*   Repair Status & Revenue Charts

## 3. Customer Management
*   Register Customer
*   Update Customer Information
*   Delete Customer
*   Search Customer
*   Customer Service History
*   **Customer Information:** Customer ID, Full Name, Phone Number, Email, Address, Registration Date.

## 4. Vehicle Management
*   Register Vehicle
*   Update Vehicle Information
*   Delete Vehicle
*   Search Vehicle
*   Upload Vehicle Photo
*   View Service History
*   **Vehicle Information:** Vehicle Number, VIN (Optional), Brand, Model, Year, Color, Engine Number, Fuel Type, Mileage, Owner.

## 5. Appointment Management
*   Book Service Appointment
*   Update Appointment
*   Cancel Appointment
*   Assign Mechanic
*   Appointment Calendar
*   **Appointment Status:** Scheduled, Confirmed, In Progress, Completed, Cancelled.

## 6. Repair Job Management
*   Create Repair Order
*   Assign Mechanic
*   Add Repair Services
*   Update Repair Progress
*   Complete Repair Job
*   Print Work Order
*   **Repair Information:** Repair Order Number, Vehicle, Customer, Problem Description, Diagnosis, Estimated Cost, Repair Status.
*   **Repair Status:** Pending, Diagnosing, Repairing, Waiting for Parts, Ready for Pickup, Completed.

## 7. Service Management
*   Add Service Type
*   Update Service
*   Delete Service
*   Search Service
*   **Example Services:** Oil Change, Engine Repair, Brake Service, Tire Replacement, Battery Replacement, Air Conditioning Repair, Wheel Alignment, Car Wash, General Inspection.

## 8. Mechanic Management
*   Add Mechanic
*   Update Mechanic Information
*   Delete Mechanic
*   Assign Repair Jobs
*   View Work Schedule
*   Track Completed Repairs
*   **Mechanic Information:** Mechanic ID, Full Name, Phone Number, Specialization, Experience, Employment Status.

## 9. Spare Parts Inventory Management
*   Add Spare Parts
*   Update Spare Parts
*   Delete Spare Parts
*   Search Inventory
*   Stock In
*   Stock Out
*   Inventory Adjustment
*   Low Stock Alert
*   **Spare Parts Information:** Part Code, Part Name, Category, Brand, Unit Price, Stock Quantity, Supplier.

## 10. Supplier Management
*   Add Supplier
*   Update Supplier
*   Delete Supplier
*   Search Supplier
*   Purchase Spare Parts
*   Supplier Purchase History

## 11. Invoice & Payment Management
*   Generate Invoice
*   Record Payment
*   Print Invoice
*   Payment History
*   Outstanding Balance
*   Refund Management
*   **Payment Methods:** Cash, Credit/Debit Card, Bank Transfer, Mobile Payment, QR Payment.

## 12. Employee Management
*   Add Employee
*   Update Employee
*   Delete Employee
*   Attendance Management
*   Work Schedule
*   Salary Information
*   **Employee Roles:** Manager, Service Advisor, Mechanic, Cashier, Storekeeper.

## 13. Maintenance History Management
*   Record Vehicle Service History
*   Record Parts Replaced
*   Record Repair Cost
*   Next Service Recommendation
*   Warranty Information

## 14. Report Management
*   **Generate reports for:** Customer Report, Vehicle Report, Repair Job Report, Mechanic Performance Report, Spare Parts Inventory Report, Supplier Report, Daily Sales Report, Monthly Revenue Report, Profit & Loss Report, Service History Report.
*   **Export:** Print, PDF, Excel.

## 15. Notification System
*   Appointment Reminder
*   Repair Completion Notification
*   Low Spare Parts Alert
*   Vehicle Ready for Pickup Notification
*   Next Service Reminder

## 16. Settings
*   Workshop Information
*   Service Pricing
*   Tax Configuration
*   Currency Settings
*   Business Hours
*   Backup Database
*   Restore Database
